Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026

tahan is a budgeting app for iPhone. Your accounts, transactions, budgets, bills, goals and loans are kept in a database on your iPhone. We don't run servers that store your data, you don't need to make an account, and there are no analytics or advertising trackers.

In short: tahan goes online for two things only. If you have an account in another currency, it downloads exchange rates. It also shows tahan Pro prices and checks your purchase. Neither one sends your transactions, balances or account names anywhere.

Exchange rates

If you keep an account in another currency, for example a USD account with US$500, tahan shows it in your SGD totals as about S$670. It gets the latest rates from the free Frankfurter service. The request only asks for rates from SGD. It does not include your amounts, merchants or any other data. Like any website, Frankfurter can see your IP address when your phone makes the request.

If all your accounts are in SGD, tahan never makes this request. When you spend abroad on an SGD card, you enter the SGD amount your bank charged, so no rates are needed.

Purchases

tahan Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never see your card or Apple ID details. Apple's privacy policy applies to that payment.

We use RevenueCat to show tahan Pro prices, check whether you have bought tahan Pro, and restore purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID created by the app, your App Store purchase records, and basic device details such as iOS version and country. It does not receive your name, email or any budgeting data.

Auto-log

Auto-log is optional. You set it up by adding a shortcut in Apple's Shortcuts app; the setup links open shortcuts hosted on Apple's iCloud. When a bank or Wallet alert arrives, for example "S$4.50 at Kopitiam", the shortcut hands that text to tahan, which logs the S$4.50 on your iPhone. tahan does not read your other notifications.

Notifications

tahan reminds you about bill, card and loan due dates, goals, budgets, payday, logging your spending and your free trial. It also confirms when auto-log records a payment. All of these are scheduled on your iPhone by iOS. None are sent from a server.

iPhone backups

If iCloud Backup is on, iOS includes tahan's data in your iPhone backup, the same as for most apps. That backup is stored by Apple under your Apple Account and Apple's privacy policy applies. You can turn this off for your whole iPhone in Settings → your name → iCloud → iCloud Backup.

Deleting your data

  • In tahan, go to Profile → Data management → Erase all data. This removes your records, profile photo, reminders and preferences.
  • Deleting the app removes its database from your iPhone.
  • The free trial start date stays in the iOS Keychain so that reinstalling doesn't restart the trial. It holds only a date.
  • Backup files you saved elsewhere stay there until you delete them.

Children

tahan isn't made for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If a change means tahan starts sending your data somewhere new, we will tell you in the app first.
